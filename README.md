# Recoverable Hindsight Regret

**Paper:** Recoverable Hindsight Regret: Calibrated Selective Repair of Trading Policies

**Authors:** Shenggang Li, Bilian Song, Yunfeng Qi, Joseph Li

## Overview

This research examines whether trading decisions that appear suboptimal in hindsight could have been improved using information available at the time.

The method combines historical policy replay, continuation-utility prediction, and calibrated selective intervention.

## Main Findings

- 17 of 24 registered specifications show positive decision-level continuation-advantage intervals under the reported bootstrap procedure.
- Evidence for improvement in continuously executed portfolio wealth remains inconclusive.
- Synthetic experiments distinguish recoverable opportunities from gains that require future information.

The results do not establish consistently profitable trading performance.

## Reproducibility

The study uses six U.S. equities, walk-forward evaluation from 2016 to 2025, and frozen trading-policy specifications.

Research code, configurations, and supporting results will be organized in this repository. Data availability and third-party redistribution restrictions will be documented.
