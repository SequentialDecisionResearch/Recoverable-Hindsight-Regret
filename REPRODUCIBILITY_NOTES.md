# Archived manuscript configurations and result tables

Source: the author-provided `latexf.zip` bundle accompanying *Recoverable Hindsight Regret: Calibrated Selective Repair of Trading Policies* (October 2026 revision).

- `configs/`: 14 YAML files defining the frozen 0–12 experiment phases and trading policies.
- `results/`: seven archived CSV tables of aggregate evaluation outputs used in the manuscript.

These files are **archived artifacts**, not evidence that this public snapshot has been independently rerun or validated. The workflow requires additional downloaded historical market data and intermediate phase outputs; results can differ when data vendors revise historical records. Price-taking, date splits, selection and bootstrap assumptions and limitations are detailed in the manuscript. Raw vendor price data are not redistributed here.

The manuscript reports descriptive positive local continuation results but does not establish positive long-run executable-path effects or a finite-sample safety certificate for multi-candidate selection. The bootstrap dependence approximation and absence of a sensitivity analysis remain limitations.

This supplement does not include all intermediate checkpoints. Complete numerical regeneration has not been verified for this GitHub snapshot.
